THE CONSTRAINT GRAMMAR OF COMPLETED INTELLIGIBILITY
Plain-Language Expositions of the Fifty-Four Generated Forms — Final Edition

These expositions approach each form as a familiar event of completion. The coordinate labels are retained for reference, but the prose asks the reader to feel the structure from inside the situation: what remains unresolved, what changes, what makes that change sufficient, and what kind of result is left. The coordinate-generated form is therefore kept distinct from its canonical crystallization, while the three established manifestations show the same event-shape recurring in different materials.

CELL 1

Differentiation / Focal / Standing

Coordinate-generated form: Focal distinguished standing

Canonical crystallization: Focal mark

Companion utterance: "This."

Constraint strength: Near-determinate

A field can contain many perfectly visible things and still leave one elementary matter unresolved: which one is meant. Nothing needs to be added to the field. What is missing is a difference that has become decisive at one place. Once one candidate is singled out, the field does not have to be reorganized everywhere; it settles around that candidate. And the result does not need a continuing relation or a further effect to keep it intelligible. The selected thing itself is now available as the achieved result. This is the smallest kind of completed differentiation: not a scale, not a sorting, not an exclusion, but one thing coming forward and remaining there as the one indicated.

The movement is compact: undifferentiated candidate → focal discrimination → distinguished standing. The opening, the decisive change, and the achieved remainder fit so closely here that the local form narrows quickly.

Crowded keyring. One key is pointed out. That key stands as the indicated one.

Many stars overhead. An astronomer singles out one star. That star stands as the one meant.

Several faces in a photograph. A finger points to one face. That person stands as the identified figure.

Across those three scenes, the materials change but the completion does not. A difference is concentrated upon one focal candidate until that candidate stands as the distinguished one.

'Focal mark' adds almost no structural content beyond the generated form. Once one candidate is differentiated, completion gathers on it, and the result simply stands, the natural local event is indication. The freedom lies mainly in what is pointed out, not in the shape of the completion.

"This."


CELL 2

Differentiation / Focal / Bearing

Coordinate-generated form: Focal contrast-bearing distinction

Canonical crystallization: Binary bearing

Companion utterance: "This, not that."

Constraint strength: Tightly constrained

Two alternatives can remain live even when both are already clearly distinguishable. The unresolvedness lies in which side will count. Completion comes when one is selected, but the rejected side does not disappear from the meaning of the choice. The selected route is this route rather than that one; the chosen setting is this setting against its opposite. The event therefore gathers at one selected locus while carrying the contrast that made the selection possible. If the alternative could simply fall away, the result would be an isolated standing. Here the completed distinction still bears its other side inside it.

The three established manifestations make the narrowness of the form visible from different sides.

Two routes remain possible. The left route is chosen over the right. The left route stands as “this, not that”.

Two similar switches offer opposite settings. One setting is selected against the other. The chosen setting stands through its contrast.

Two finalists remain. One candidate is selected rather than the other. The winner stands with the rejected alternative as defining opposite.

Seen whole, the event moves from live alternatives, through focal selection, into contrast-bearing standing. What is fixed is the structure of that movement; what remains available for semantic sharpening is the particular local way the structure is realized.

The coordinate-generated sentence states the required event without committing to one manifestation:

One focal alternative is distinguished while its completed standing remains constitutively defined by the alternative against which it was selected.

The generated form requires a focally selected distinction whose meaning retains its contrasting alternative. 'Binary bearing' sharpens that by making the surviving contrast explicitly two-sided. The coordinate does not require every possible contrast to be literally binary, but it strongly directs us toward selection-under-opposition. This is why the form is Tightly constrained rather than fully forced: the generated region is narrow, but the canonical term still contributes a meaningful specification.

"This, not that."


CELL 3

Differentiation / Focal / Efficacy

Coordinate-generated form: Focal operative distinction

Canonical crystallization: Boundary act

Companion utterance: "This far."

Constraint strength: Open realization

Sometimes a difference matters because it changes what can happen at one point. A channel may continue, a runner may keep moving, a line may keep extending. The unresolved question is where continuation will cease to count as continuation. Once one locus is made decisive, that difference is not merely noticed: it takes effect. The gate stops the water; the finish line ends the run; the marked point terminates the stroke. The coordinate requires a focal distinction with consequence. It does not by itself require that consequence to be a boundary, which is why the familiar form that emerges here is more specific than the coordinate alone.

Its inner course can be stated simply: undifferentiated continuation → operative focal distinction → changed continuation. The coordinate fixes that deep course, but it does not yet decide every semantic detail of the crystallization.

Water runs freely along a channel. A gate is lowered at one point. The flow stops there.

A runner continues along an open course. The finish line fixes the endpoint. The run ends at that line.

A pencil stroke extends across the page. The hand stops at a marked point. The line terminates there.

The common structure can therefore be stated more broadly than any one of those scenes:

A focal difference becomes operative so that one determinate locus changes what can continue or occur.

The coordinate requires an operative difference concentrated at one locus. 'Boundary act' adds the specific idea that the efficacy is stopping or terminating continuation. Other focal operative distinctions are possible in principle, so the boundary belongs to crystallization rather than to the bare coordinate. That distinction is the point of calling this an Open realization: the coordinate determines a recognizable phenomenological syntax, while the canonical crystallization chooses one especially lucid semantic member of that wider admissible region.

"This far."


CELL 4

Differentiation / Distributed / Standing

Coordinate-generated form: Distributed distinguished standing

Canonical crystallization: Scope standing

Companion utterance: "Within this."

Constraint strength: Tightly constrained

A scope is not made by pointing to one thing. It appears when a difference is made to hold over an extent. Before the change, the woodland, workshop, or discussion has no settled inside for the purpose at hand. A criterion is then carried through a region, and the region itself becomes the completed result. One point would not be enough: the distinction has to cover what is to count as inside. Once it does, what remains is simply the differentiated extent—there to be taken up as the relevant scope.

The three established manifestations make the narrowness of the form visible from different sides.

Open woodland with no study boundary. Researchers mark one plot and apply the study criterion within it. That plot stands as the protected study region.

A workshop floor with mixed activity. One marked area is designated for assembly. That area stands as the assembly zone.

A long discussion ranging over many issues. The group fixes the present question as the operative scope. That question stands as the region within which discussion counts.

At full compression, the change is undelimited field → criterion distributed across an extent → differentiated region standing. What is fixed is the structure of that movement; what remains available for semantic sharpening is the particular local way the structure is realized.

The coordinate-generated sentence states the required event without committing to one manifestation:

A criterion is made to hold across a plurality or extent until a differentiated region or arrangement stands as such.

The coordinate requires a distributed difference whose achieved extent stands. 'Scope standing' specifies that the standing is a region of applicability—an inside. That is strongly guided, though not the only conceivable distributed differentiated standing. This is why the form is Tightly constrained rather than fully forced: the generated region is narrow, but the canonical term still contributes a meaningful specification.

"Within this."


CELL 5

Differentiation / Distributed / Bearing

Coordinate-generated form: Context-bearing distinction

Canonical crystallization: Context-bound mark

Companion utterance: "This here."

Constraint strength: Tightly constrained

A mark can be perfectly clear and still make sense only here. The dealer token is special for this round; the theatre door is special for tonight; the map symbol is special at the reader's present position. The difference is therefore not carried by the object alone. It is made within a wider arrangement, and that arrangement remains part of what the distinction means. The completion is distributed because the surrounding field supplies the conditions under which the mark counts, and it persists as bearing because the mark cannot be detached from that context without changing its identity.

The three established manifestations make the narrowness of the form visible from different sides.

Identical red tokens used in several games. One token is marked as the dealer token for this round. Its distinction stands only within that game context.

Several doors look alike in a theatre corridor. A temporary sign marks one as the actors’ entrance for tonight. That door stands as distinct within the current performance context.

Repeated symbols appear across a map. One symbol is highlighted as “you are here” at the current location. That mark stands through its local context.

The form's own movement is equivalent occurrences → context-indexed distinction → context-bearing standing. What is fixed is the structure of that movement; what remains available for semantic sharpening is the particular local way the structure is realized.

The coordinate-generated sentence states the required event without committing to one manifestation:

Something becomes distinct within a wider field, but its completed distinction remains inseparable from the contextual relations through which it counts there.

The generated form already requires a distinction that remains dependent on the wider field through which it counts. 'Context-bound mark' names that dependence as contextual indexing. It is a close sharpening rather than an arbitrary choice. This is why the form is Tightly constrained rather than fully forced: the generated region is narrow, but the canonical term still contributes a meaningful specification.

"This here."


CELL 6

Differentiation / Distributed / Efficacy

Coordinate-generated form: Distributed differential efficacy

Canonical crystallization: Field separation

Companion utterance: "These among these."

Constraint strength: Near-determinate

A mixed field is not resolved by finding one interesting member. The same difference has to be applied through the range until the field itself is reorganized. Sorting recyclables, assigning runners to age groups, and separating viable seeds all require the criterion to travel through the plurality. The result is not merely that differences are known; the differences now govern treatment. Items go into different streams, groups, or sets. The completed distinction therefore exists as something the field can now do with its members.

What has to happen, in order, is mixed plurality → distributed discrimination → operative separation. The opening, the decisive change, and the achieved remainder fit so closely here that the local form narrows quickly.

Mixed recyclables move along a sorting belt. Scanners discriminate plastic from the rest across the stream. Plastic items are operatively separated.

Runners of several age groups gather at registration. Officials sort competitors across the field by age category. Distinct race groups are produced.

Mixed seeds cover a sorting tray. The gardener separates viable seeds from damaged ones across the batch. Two operatively distinct sets result.

Across those three scenes, the materials change but the completion does not. A distinguishing criterion is carried through a plurality until it produces an operative separation or differential treatment across the field.

Once a criterion differentiates across a plurality and leaves an operative consequence, 'Field separation' follows with little remainder. The exact sorting mechanism and domain may vary; the structural event is nearly fixed.

"These among these."


CELL 7

Differentiation / Release / Standing

Coordinate-generated form: Released differentiated standing

Canonical crystallization: Opposed standing

Companion utterance: "Opposed."

Constraint strength: Tightly constrained

Two positions can be present without yet being allowed to stand apart. The unresolved pressure is a common holding that keeps their difference from taking its own shape. Completion arrives when that common governance is broken and the positions can stand independently. The coordinate requires released differentiation: the earlier conflation no longer decides the field. The canonical form sharpens this into opposition. That sharpening matters, because separation alone would satisfy the broader generated syntax; standing over against one another is the more specific crystallization.

The three established manifestations make the narrowness of the form visible from different sides.

Two teams treated as one undifferentiated crowd. They are set against each other as opponents. Two opposed sides stand.

Two magnetic poles considered without relation. They are oriented into opposition. Opposed polar standings become clear.

Two arguments blended together in discussion. Their claims are separated and placed against one another. Two opposed positions stand.

The movement is compact: conflated positions → release into differentiation → distinct standings. What is fixed is the structure of that movement; what remains available for semantic sharpening is the particular local way the structure is realized.

The coordinate-generated sentence states the required event without committing to one manifestation:

Previously conflated possibilities are released from common governance so that differentiated positions can stand independently.

The coordinates require differentiated standings made possible by release from conflation. 'Opposed standing' adds that the separated positions face one another as contraries. That is a significant but strongly guided sharpening. This is why the form is Tightly constrained rather than fully forced: the generated region is narrow, but the canonical term still contributes a meaningful specification.

"Opposed."


CELL 8

Differentiation / Release / Bearing

Coordinate-generated form: Released comparative bearing

Canonical crystallization: Graded contrast

Companion utterance: "More and less."

Constraint strength: Tightly constrained

Variation can be real yet flattened. Several temperatures, speeds, or shades may differ without those differences being organized as more and less. The change occurs when the flat holding is released and the members take places in a comparative order. No single member is enough to carry the result, because every position is intelligible through its relation to others: hotter than, slower than, darker than. The completed difference therefore persists as bearing. The coordinate nearly demands comparison, while the canonical crystallization selects gradation as the particularly natural form that comparison takes.

The three established manifestations make the narrowness of the form visible from different sides.

Several cups seem simply “hot”. Their temperatures are ordered from cooler to hotter. A more-and-less gradient stands.

Runners finish with different speeds. Their performances are ranked by pace. A graded contrast from faster to slower stands.

Paint samples look like similar shades. They are arranged from lightest to darkest. A continuous gradient of difference stands.

Seen whole, the event moves from flattened variation, through release into ordered difference, into comparative bearing. What is fixed is the structure of that movement; what remains available for semantic sharpening is the particular local way the structure is realized.

The coordinate-generated sentence states the required event without committing to one manifestation:

A previously flattened or commonly held difference is released into a differentiated arrangement whose members remain intelligible by their relative bearing.

The coordinate requires released differentiation whose completed identity is comparative. 'Graded contrast' specifies that the comparison takes ordered degrees rather than some other relative structure. The atlas examples make that sharpening especially natural. This is why the form is Tightly constrained rather than fully forced: the generated region is narrow, but the canonical term still contributes a meaningful specification.

"More and less."


CELL 9

Differentiation / Release / Efficacy

Coordinate-generated form: Released exclusionary efficacy

Canonical crystallization: Exclusive cut

Companion utterance: "Not admitted."

Constraint strength: Tightly constrained

Admission has a peculiar structure: a criterion does not merely describe candidates; it changes what may pass. At first, several candidates are still live before the threshold. Completion comes when the shared admissibility is broken. What fails the criterion is no longer governed as though it remained equally eligible, and the distinction takes effect in what proceeds. The result is therefore not simply a standing classification. It is an exclusion with consequences. This makes the local form much narrower than a generic act of differentiation.

The three established manifestations make the narrowness of the form visible from different sides.

Two people both approach a single-seat kayak. One is admitted to take the seat and the other is excluded. Only one occupant proceeds.

Two keys fit partway into a lock. The correct key is accepted and the other rejected. Only the admitted key operates the lock.

Several entries reach a members-only gate. Membership is checked and nonmembers are refused. Admission proceeds by operative exclusion.

Its inner course can be stated simply: shared admissibility → release by criterion → operative exclusion. What is fixed is the structure of that movement; what remains available for semantic sharpening is the particular local way the structure is realized.

The coordinate-generated sentence states the required event without committing to one manifestation:

A distinction becomes complete by releasing what fails its criterion from prior admissibility, producing an effective separation in what may proceed.

The generated form already points toward a criterion that removes some candidates from what may proceed. 'Exclusive cut' gives that operative removal a concise local name. The coordinate leaves little room beyond how exclusion is implemented. This is why the form is Tightly constrained rather than fully forced: the generated region is narrow, but the canonical term still contributes a meaningful specification.

"Not admitted."


CELL 10

Opening / Focal / Standing

Coordinate-generated form: Focal available field

Canonical crystallization: Source field

Companion utterance: "From here."

Constraint strength: Near-determinate

An opening can gather around a source. Before the lamp is switched on, before a beacon becomes an origin, before a speaker takes the microphone, the surrounding field lacks a point from which availability spreads. The change is not merely that one object is selected; what becomes available around it is enlarged. Completion is achieved when the field can be taken up from that source, and what remains is the opened field itself. Source and field belong together so tightly here that the crystallization is almost already contained in the generated form.

At full compression, the change is constrained availability → focal source opening → available field. The opening, the decisive change, and the achieved remainder fit so closely here that the local form narrows quickly.

A dark room with little visible. One lamp is switched on at the center. A visible field opens from that source.

A map with no local reference point. One beacon is fixed as the origin. The surrounding area becomes intelligible from that point.

A crowd waits without knowing where instructions come from. One speaker takes the microphone. An audible field gathers around that source.

Across those three scenes, the materials change but the completion does not. Availability opens around one privileged source or centre until an enlarged field stands as accessible from there.

'Source field' is almost simply the coordinate-generated form compressed. Focal opening produces an available field from a privileged source, and Standing leaves that field there as achieved.

"From here."


CELL 11

Opening / Focal / Bearing

Coordinate-generated form: Source-bearing divergence

Canonical crystallization: Source-carried divergence

Companion utterance: "From here, either way."

Constraint strength: Near-determinate

One source can give rise to more than one way forward without ceasing to be the source of all of them. A fork, a branching phrase, or a manifold does not merely add options somewhere in the field; the divergence gathers at one origin. And the branches remain intelligible as branches of that origin. Their source relation survives the opening. The result therefore differs both from a bare list of available options and from an active branching mechanism: what remains is a set of continuations carrying their common beginning with them.

The form's own movement is single continuation → focal opening into alternatives → source-bearing divergence. The opening, the decisive change, and the achieved remainder fit so closely here that the local form narrows quickly.

One trail reaches a mountain fork. Two paths open from the same junction. Divergent routes remain linked to one source.

One melody reaches a branching phrase. The composer develops two different continuations from it. Both musical paths bear their common origin.

One central pipe reaches a manifold. Flow is divided into several source-linked branches. Multiple paths extend from the same supply.

Across those three scenes, the materials change but the completion does not. Several possibilities open from one focal source while each completed continuation remains intelligible through its bearing back to that common origin.

The coordinate practically supplies the phrase: multiple openings from one source remain source-related. 'Source-carried divergence' only tightens the wording of a form that is already nearly determinate.

"From here, either way."


CELL 12

Opening / Focal / Efficacy

Coordinate-generated form: Focal generative opening

Canonical crystallization: Branching output

Companion utterance: "More ways open."

Constraint strength: Tightly constrained

A point can do more than stand as an origin; it can become productive. The opening pressure is a single continuation or restricted possibility-set. At one locus the field changes so that multiple continuations become operative. Buds split a stem, a question opens lines of inquiry, switches send trains onto outgoing tracks. The completion is focal because branching is organized at the source point, and efficacious because the new possibilities are not merely conceivable—they are now generated paths of continuation.

The three established manifestations make the narrowness of the form visible from different sides.

One young stem grows upward. Buds split its growth into several shoots. Multiple growing branches result.

One initial question guides a lesson. The teacher opens several lines of inquiry from it. Multiple avenues of investigation grow.

One railway line reaches a switching yard. Switches send trains onto several outgoing tracks. Multiple continuations become operative.

What has to happen, in order, is closed possibility set → focal generative opening → multiple operative continuations. What is fixed is the structure of that movement; what remains available for semantic sharpening is the particular local way the structure is realized.

The coordinate-generated sentence states the required event without committing to one manifestation:

A focal point of opening actively generates multiple new continuations or possibilities.

The coordinate fixes focal opening as productive efficacy. 'Branching output' chooses branching as the local image of that production. Other kinds of focal generative opening remain imaginable, so the name adds a real but narrow sharpening. This is why the form is Tightly constrained rather than fully forced: the generated region is narrow, but the canonical term still contributes a meaningful specification.

"More ways open."


CELL 13

Opening / Distributed / Standing

Canonical crystallization: Alternate passage

Companion utterance: "Another way through."

Constraint strength: Tightly constrained

When the usual way through is blocked, an opening is not complete merely because a new point becomes available. Passage has to be re-established through an intervening field. The side road, connecting corridor, and higher trail all solve the same problem: the field becomes traversable again by another route. What remains after the change is not an ongoing mechanism but a passage that stands open. The canonical crystallization adds the idea of alternation to a broader coordinate demand for distributed passage standing.

The three established manifestations make the narrowness of the form visible from different sides.

The main road is blocked. Traffic is redirected along a side route. Another way through stands open.

A hallway is closed for repairs. People cross the building by a connecting corridor. An alternate passage remains available.

The usual hiking trail is flooded. Walkers take a higher path around the water. Passage continues by another route.

The movement is compact: blocked traversal → distributed opening across a field → passage standing. What is fixed is the structure of that movement; what remains available for semantic sharpening is the particular local way the structure is realized.

The coordinate-generated sentence states the required event without committing to one manifestation:

Availability is enlarged through traversal of a plurality or intervening field until an additional passage or route stands open.

Distributed opening that remains as a passage strongly suggests another way through. 'Alternate passage' adds the semantic relation to a blocked or usual route. The coordinate is narrow but does not logically require alternation. This is why the form is Tightly constrained rather than fully forced: the generated region is narrow, but the canonical term still contributes a meaningful specification.

"Another way through."


CELL 14

Opening / Distributed / Bearing

Coordinate-generated form: Span-bearing reach

Canonical crystallization: Span-carried reach

Companion utterance: "Reachable across."

Constraint strength: Near-determinate

Distance is the important unresolvedness here. Something can exist at the far side and still not be available from here. The opening must succeed through the interval itself. A bridge, cable, or suspension span enlarges reach only by maintaining a connection across what separates the two positions. That connection cannot disappear from the completed meaning: the far side remains reachable because the carrier remains. Opening, distributed sufficiency, and bearing therefore fuse into one unusually narrow event-shape.

Seen whole, the event moves from local availability, through span-maintaining connection, into distant reach. The opening, the decisive change, and the achieved remainder fit so closely here that the local form narrows quickly.

A village lies beyond a river. A bridge carries passage across the gap. The far bank remains reachable.

A computer sits far from the router. A network cable spans the distance. Connection is maintained at the remote machine.

Two cliffs are separated by a ravine. A suspension bridge links the span. The opposite side stays within reach.

Across those three scenes, the materials change but the completion does not. Something previously local becomes available at a separated position because a connection maintains reach across the intervening span.

Here generation and crystallization nearly coincide. Opening must cross a span, and the achieved reach remains through its carrier. 'Span-carried reach' is little more than the event's natural name.

"Reachable across."

A resource may be available and yet too local to count as available for the field that needs it. The tank, access point, or depot is not absent; the problem is concentration. And what remains is practical efficacy: water, network access, or supplies can now be used throughout the field.

Water is available only at one tank. Irrigation lines distribute outlets across the field. Water becomes available throughout the crops.

Across those three scenes, the materials change but the completion does not. An initially localized availability is distributed through a wider field until access becomes operative throughout that plurality.

Distributed Opening plus Efficacy almost demands availability throughout a field. 'Field distribution' is therefore a compressed name for a structure the coordinates have already specified.

"Available throughout."


CELL 16

Opening / Release / Standing

Coordinate-generated form: Released option standing

Canonical crystallization: Released option

Companion utterance: "Free to choose."

Constraint strength: Near-determinate

Freedom of choice begins when compulsion stops governing. The second route, project topic, or machine mode does not have to be created from nothing; it has to become genuinely available. The decisive change is therefore both an opening and a release. Once the old restriction loses its authority, an option simply stands. Nothing more has to operate for the form to be complete. The possibility is there, and it is live in a way it was not before.

At full compression, the change is governed restriction → release into alternative → option standing. The opening, the decisive change, and the achieved remainder fit so closely here that the local form narrows quickly.

A hiker is required to follow one marked trail. The restriction is lifted and a second route is opened. An alternative stands available for choice.

A machine is locked to one operating mode. The lock is released and another mode becomes selectable. An alternative operating state stands available.

Across those three scenes, the materials change but the completion does not. A prior restriction loses its governance, leaving an additional possibility standing as genuinely available.

A possibility becomes available because restriction ceases to govern, and the possibility simply stands. 'Released option' is the natural contraction of that event; very little semantic choice remains.

"Free to choose."

A prior interruption or segmentation loses its governance so that enlarged continuity persists through an unbroken bearing.

CELL 19

Relating / Focal / Standing

Coordinate-generated form: Focal relation standing

Canonical crystallization: Direct bearing

Companion utterance: "This bears on that."

Constraint strength: Near-determinate

Two terms can coexist without a relation between them being part of the situation. The rope between boats, the phone call between people, and the wire between sensor and alarm each establish one privileged connection. Once that connection is made, nothing else has to spread through a larger field for the relation to count as complete. The relation itself is simply there. This is why direct bearing is nearly forced: the transformation is relational, the completion gathers at one link, and the residue is the relation standing as achieved.

The movement is compact: unrelated terms → focal connection → relation standing. The opening, the decisive change, and the achieved remainder fit so closely here that the local form narrows quickly.

A loose rope lies between two boats. The rope is tied directly from one to the other. A direct connection stands between them.

Two people have no contact. One calls the other by phone. A direct relation stands between them.

A sensor and alarm are separate. The sensor is wired straight to the alarm. The sensor now bears directly on the alarm.

Across those three scenes, the materials change but the completion does not. A bearing is established directly around a focal connection until the relation itself stands as determinate.

A focal relation is established and simply stands. 'Direct bearing' is almost forced because the relation's focality is most naturally realized as one direct connection.

"This bears on that."


CELL 20

Relating / Focal / Bearing

Coordinate-generated form: Focal counterbearing

Canonical crystallization: Pivoted balance

Companion utterance: "Held in counterbalance."

Constraint strength: Open realization

A relation can settle around a centre without leaving a single self-standing result. In a seesaw, mobile, or scale, the important thing is how opposing tendencies are held around a pivot. The field becomes complete when the focal relation organizes those tendencies into a maintained counterbearing. Remove the relation of counterweight and the result disappears. The coordinate therefore demands a focal relational bearing, but it does not uniquely demand balance or a physical pivot. 'Pivoted balance' is a vivid realization of a broader region in which focal relation persists through constitutive counterrelation.
