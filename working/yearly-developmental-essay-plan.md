# Working Plan: Developmental Editions, Ages 6–18

**Status:** Working editorial-development document. Not canonical theory.

## Purpose

Plan thirteen new essays, one for each developmental year from **6 through 18**, each approximately **3,000 words**, each functioning as a complete higher-resolution re-presentation of the same theory already present in the Five-Year-Old Edition.

These are not thirteen chapters that divide the theory into topics. They are thirteen **recursive passes through the whole system**.

> **Year 5 = the whole theory at developmental resolution 5.**  
> **Year 6 = the whole theory at developmental resolution 6.**  
> **Year 7 = the whole theory at developmental resolution 7.**  
> …  
> **Year 18 = the whole theory at developmental resolution 18.**

The actual readership is adult throughout. The ages govern conceptual and linguistic load, not the literal identity of the reader. The essays must never perform childhood or condescend. They should instead show how far the complete system can be articulated truthfully at each developmental resolution.

The repository governs all terminology, examples, theoretical distinctions, intellectual history, academic positioning, and claims boundaries.

---

# I. Governing principle: lossless recursive deepening

Every later essay inherits every earlier achievement. No year owns a theoretical topic exclusively.

Each essay should:

1. retain what has already been understood;
2. traverse the complete theoretical architecture again;
3. use substantially the same corpus examples and rhetorical furniture;
4. expose one or two limitations in the previous resolution;
5. sharpen those points without invalidating the earlier account;
6. redescribe the whole system at the newly available resolution;
7. end by creating the pressure that the next year can answer.

The governing standard is **lossless simplification**. The mature account should be able to look back at every earlier account and say: “yes — and now we can see more precisely what that meant.”

This means every major idea develops across the full age range. For example, coordinate-generated form is not “saved” for one later essay. At 5 it is the event made by answering three questions; at 8 it is a recurring event-shape across different materials; at 11 it is explicitly distinguished from name and example; at 15 it can be generated prospectively; at 18 it is stated in mature formal and epistemic terms.

The same principle applies to every component below.

---

# II. Stable furniture of every yearly essay

Every 3,000-word edition should make a complete pass through these recurring elements.

## 1. Structured incompletion

Begin with familiar corpus situations that are unfinished in a particular way. Revisit **opening pressure** at the resolution available to that year.

## 2. What changes

Revisit all six **Transformation Patterns** every year:

- Differentiation
- Opening
- Relating
- Repair
- Integration
- Settlement

## 3. What makes the change enough

Revisit all three **Completion Topologies** every year:

- Focal
- Distributed
- Release

## 4. What remains afterward

Revisit all three **Persistence Modes** every year:

- Standing
- Bearing
- Efficacy

## 5. The complete combinatorial architecture

Revisit **6 × 3 × 3 = 54 positions** every year. Early essays treat this as a comprehensible combinatorial grammar; later essays articulate functional independence, periodicity, state-space structure, and constrained composition.

## 6. Coordinate construction

Every year should build at least one coordinate from the three axes and inspect what kind of event it produces.

## 7. Generated form, canonical crystallization, manifestation

Every year should preserve this hierarchy at an age-appropriate level:

> **coordinate-generated form → canonical crystallization → concrete manifestation**

## 8. Intelligibility before truth

Every year should retain the distinction between something becoming determinate enough to judge and its being true, correct, justified, successful, or good.

## 9. Completed form and formation history

Every year should retain the idea that completion can be local, nested, revisable, or later reopened. As language matures, this develops into historical formation, local/global completion, governing transformation, landing, reanalysis, and persistent result.

## 10. What kind of theory this is

Even early editions should avoid quietly making the system into a theory of objects, truth, language, or finality. The explanatory level becomes progressively explicit until the mature functional-phenomenological and cognitive-scientific positioning is available.

## 11. What the theory is entitled to claim

Every year ends with an age-appropriate claims boundary. By 18 this becomes the full Research Status distinction between internal demonstration and external validation.

# III. Stable 3,000-word architecture

A useful recurring distribution is:

- opening situation and opening pressure: **300–400**
- six transformations: **350–450**
- three topologies: **250–350**
- three persistence modes: **250–350**
- 54-position grammar and coordinate walkthrough: **500–600**
- generated form / crystallization / manifestations: **350–450**
- formation, truth, theory-level, claims boundary: **500–600**
- closing bridge: **100–200**

The section headings may vary. The important invariant is that the **whole system is articulated every year**.

# IV. Developmental progression: whole-system articulation by year

## Year 5 — Existing baseline

**Role:** The complete skeleton at primitive resolution.

The existing edition already presents structured unfinishedness, the six transformations, three topologies, three persistence modes, the 54 positions, coordinate generation, form/name/example, intelligibility before truth, and nested completion. Its distinctive achievement is not incompleteness but **breadth at very low abstraction**.

---

## Year 6 — Whole system as particular problems receiving enough of an answer

**Placeholder:** _The Six-Year-Old Edition_

The whole theory is redescribed through the idea that different unfinished situations need different kinds of answers.

- **Opening pressure:** what this particular situation is still waiting for.
- **Transformations:** six different kinds of needed change.
- **Topologies:** one place can make the change enough; many places together can make it enough; or the thing holding the problem in place can stop doing so.
- **Persistence:** the answer can remain as something there, something held by a relation, or something that now works/changes what can happen.
- **54 positions:** three questions combine into many possible kinds of completed event.
- **Generated form:** the event made by the three answers together.
- **Crystallization/manifestation:** a useful name is not the same thing as the shape, and a real example is not the name.
- **Formation:** one part can finish before the whole thing does.
- **Truth:** a clear answer can still be wrong.
- **Theory level:** this is about ways situations become answerable, not about one subject matter.
- **Claims:** describe recurring patterns without saying everything must fit them.

**New sharpening:** stopping versus answering the particular unfinished demand.

---

## Year 7 — Whole system as three genuinely different questions

**Placeholder:** _The Seven-Year-Old Edition_

- **Opening pressure:** the kind of unfinishedness already points toward the kind of change needed.
- **Transformations:** what kind of problem changed.
- **Topologies:** how that change became enough is kept separate from what changed.
- **Release:** the old way the situation was being held no longer controls it.
- **Persistence:** what remains is a third question, not part of the first two.
- **54 positions:** combinations of different kinds of answer, not 54 unrelated items.
- **Generated form:** each answer contributes something different to the resulting event.
- **Crystallization/manifestation:** labels help memory but do not create structure.
- **Formation:** the way something got finished and what kind of finished result it is are not quite the same question.
- **Truth:** intelligibility supplies something definite enough to disagree with.
- **Theory level:** same structure can recur in unlike materials.
- **Claims:** internal distinctions are not yet claims about how every mind works.

**New sharpening:** what changed ≠ what made it enough ≠ how the result remains.

---

## Year 8 — Whole system as recurring structure across different materials

**Placeholder:** _The Eight-Year-Old Edition_

- **Opening pressure:** grouped by recurring kinds of unresolvedness.
- **Transformations:** understood as invariants across keys, roads, mechanisms, decisions, language, and other corpus examples.
- **Topologies:** compared across several Transformation Patterns rather than learned from one example each.
- **Persistence:** one object or event can be redescribed as Standing, Bearing, or Efficacy depending on what remains relevant.
- **54 positions:** increasingly a structured space rather than a multiplication result.
- **Generated form:** an event-shape that can recur across different contents.
- **Crystallization/manifestation:** the same structural form can have several material examples.
- **Formation:** local/global completion becomes a repeatable distinction.
- **Truth:** false stories, plans, explanations, and diagnoses can still have determinate form.
- **Theory level:** subject matter and completion structure are clearly different.
- **Claims:** recurrence in corpus examples is illustrative/internal evidence, not universality.

**New sharpening:** identify what stays the same when subject matter changes.

---

## Year 9 — Whole system through invariants and substitution

**Placeholder:** _The Nine-Year-Old Edition_

- **Opening pressure:** linked more tightly to Transformation Pattern as its corresponding kind of unresolved demand.
- **Transformations / Topologies / Persistence:** test each contribution by holding it fixed while varying the other two.
- **54 positions:** read in small families of neighboring cells.
- **Generated form:** understood as the conjunction of three recurring contributions.
- **Crystallization:** no longer treated as the definition of the coordinate.
- **Manifestation:** cross-domain variation becomes a way to see invariance.
- **Formation:** comparable completed structures can arise through richer histories.
- **Truth:** the structural question remains prior to endorsement.
- **Theory level:** functional distinctions inside the grammar become visible.
- **Claims:** distinguish “independent contribution inside the grammar” from stronger empirical independence.

**New sharpening:** recurrence under substitution.

---

## Year 10 — Whole system as constraint grammar

**Placeholder:** _The Ten-Year-Old Edition_

- **Opening pressure:** constrains what kind of transformation could answer the situation.
- **Three axes:** understood as compositional roles rather than lists.
- **54 positions:** explicitly a grammar rather than a catalogue.
- **Generated form:** the three coordinate values jointly constrain an event-shape before its familiar name is consulted.
- **Crystallization:** downstream semantic sharpening.
- **Manifestation:** evidence that one structural pattern can recur across contents.
- **Formation:** distinguished in outline from completed coordinate.
- **Truth:** completion creates something available for judgment, not a guarantee of correctness.
- **Theory level:** the object is completion structure.
- **Claims:** internal generation does not prove cognitive or metaphysical necessity.

**New sharpening:** classification versus generation.

---

## Year 11 — Whole system with explicit representational levels

**Placeholder:** _The Eleven-Year-Old Edition_

- **Opening pressure:** increasingly described as structured incompletion.
- **Three axes:** provide structural constraints.
- **54 positions:** each coordinate defines a local generated region.
- **Generated form:** the primary local theoretical object.
- **Canonical crystallization:** selected stable semantic realization.
- **Concrete manifestation:** particular instance in material.
- **Structural determination / semantic individuation:** begin to be named.
- **Formation:** historical path remains separate from local completed form.
- **Truth:** determinate uptake is separated from epistemic endorsement.
- **Theory level:** representational levels become explicit.
- **Claims:** canonical names are not necessary essences.

**New sharpening:** structure ≠ canonical name ≠ concrete case.

---

## Year 12 — Whole system with graded constraint strength

**Placeholder:** _The Twelve-Year-Old Edition_

- **Opening pressure:** constrains but need not uniquely determine later semantic realization.
- **Three axes:** jointly narrow what can count at a coordinate.
- **54 positions:** structurally complete while differing in semantic width.
- **Generated form:** defines an admissible region.
- **Constraint strength:** Near-determinate, Tightly constrained, Open realization.
- **Semantic freedom:** bounded latitude rather than arbitrariness.
- **Crystallization:** can add more or less semantic determination depending on the coordinate.
- **Manifestation:** multiple legitimate realizations can share a generated form.
- **Formation:** remains independent of constraint width.
- **Truth:** semantic latitude does not erase determinacy.
- **Claims:** structural determination is not empirical uniqueness.

**New sharpening:** degrees of determination.

---

## Year 13 — Whole system with completed form separated from historical formation

**Placeholder:** _The Thirteen-Year-Old Edition_

- **Opening pressure:** both structural incompletion and the opening condition of a historical event.
- **Three axes:** characterize completed organization, not mandatory temporal stages.
- **54 positions:** a state-space of completed forms, not histories.
- **Generated form:** compressed structural result.
- **Formation history:** opening, local operations/completions, governing transformation, landing, persistent result.
- **Sentences:** privileged observational case because their unfolding exposes formation.
- **Crystallization/manifestation:** remain downstream of coordinate form, not products of sentence chronology.
- **Truth:** remains distinct from how an interpretation lands.
- **Theory level:** sentence processing is not the ontology of the grammar.
- **Claims:** a completed coordinate does not automatically imply one uniquely recoverable formation history.

**New sharpening:** achieved organization versus path of formation.

---

## Year 14 — Whole system as periodic state-space

**Placeholder:** _The Fourteen-Year-Old Edition_

- **Opening pressure and six transformations:** six recurring families generate six nine-position blocks.
- **Topologies:** repeat as a three-position rhythm within each family.
- **Persistence:** repeats as Standing/Bearing/Efficacy within each topology.
- **54 positions:** a finite nested periodic state-space.
- **Generated form:** periodicity appears in functional sentence roles, not merely repeated labels.
- **Crystallizations:** can be compared against structural expectations generated by their coordinates.
- **Manifestations:** demonstrate recurrence without defining periodicity.
- **Formation:** remains orthogonal to the Atlas’s completed-form organization.
- **Truth:** not supplied by formal position.
- **Theory level:** light formalism becomes legitimate.
- **Claims:** finite and periodic does not mean metaphysically exhaustive or fully axiomatized.

**New sharpening:** nested periodicity.

---

## Year 15 — Whole system under prospective generation

**Placeholder:** _The Fifteen-Year-Old Edition_

- **Opening pressure:** helps identify the relevant Transformation constraint.
- **Three axes:** composed deliberately rather than recovered only after examples.
- **54 positions:** become a generative search-space.
- **Generated form:** written prospectively as a domain-general event-sentence.
- **Admissible region:** the family of realizations satisfying all three coordinate constraints.
- **Constraint strength:** predicts internal semantic width, not external empirical probability.
- **Crystallization/manifestation:** revealed after generation as checks and realizations.
- **Formation:** historical route remains distinct from prospective structural generation.
- **Truth:** generated intelligibility remains separable from truth.
- **Theory level:** prospective generation strengthens theoretical status beyond mnemonic taxonomy.
- **Claims:** internal derivation must not be called empirical prediction without testing.

**New sharpening:** prospective rather than merely retrospective use of the grammar.

## Year 16 — Whole system with its intellectual history visible

**Placeholder:** _The Sixteen-Year-Old Edition_

- **Opening pressure / Transformation:** mature terms govern; early Work vocabulary is reconstructed historically.
- **Topologies:** Toward/Across/Away are shown as precursors to Focal/Distributed/Release, with Release reinterpreted as de-governance rather than direction.
- **Persistence:** Is/With/Does are shown as precursors to Standing/Bearing/Efficacy.
- **54 positions:** the 6×3×3 skeleton survived the theory’s reinterpretation.
- **Generated form:** emerges as more fundamental than named atlas cells.
- **Crystallization/manifestation:** clarify why the early atlas looked classificatory.
- **Formation:** sentence resolution is reconstructed as the epistemic route through which the architecture was discovered.
- **Truth:** retains its earlier separation.
- **Theory level:** shift from sentence-resolution atlas to constraint-compositional theory of completed intelligibility.
- **Claims:** historical development explains the theory’s reinterpretation; it does not prove its universality.

**New sharpening:** the theory’s own change of explanatory level.

## Year 17 — Whole system in the academic landscape

**Placeholder:** _The Seventeen-Year-Old Edition_

- **Object:** recurrent structural forms by which structured incompletion becomes sufficiently complete for determinate uptake.
- **Three axes:** stated in mature functional language.
- **54 positions:** finite compositional architecture.
- **Generated form:** domain-general phenomenological event-syntax prior to crystallization.
- **Crystallization/manifestation:** preserve the theory’s explanatory level across applications.
- **Formation:** process-sensitive without becoming a general process ontology.
- **Truth:** intelligibility is prior to epistemic endorsement but is not identical with understanding in the normative epistemological sense.
- **Academic location:** functional-phenomenological theory of completion structure, best currently situated in theoretical cognitive science / philosophy of cognitive science, with phenomenology as closest descriptive neighbor.
- **Neighbors:** linguistics, cognitive psychology, systems theory, AI/computation, epistemology, and related fields are compared without assimilation.
- **Mechanism boundary:** the theory does not currently specify neural implementation, algorithms, learning rules, or full axiomatization.
- **Claims:** no independent discipline is yet warranted.

**New sharpening:** explanatory level and disciplinary location.

## Year 18 — Whole system with full formal and epistemic self-understanding

**Placeholder:** _The Eighteen-Year-Old Edition_

This is the complete mature traversal. Nothing important is withheld linguistically or conceptually.

- **Completed intelligibility:** sufficiently determinate relative to governing opening pressure to support stable next uptake without owing the same unresolved work.
- **Opening pressure:** structured incompletion / negative contour of the completion to come.
- **Transformation Pattern:** six mature functional definitions.
- **Completion Topology:** Focal concentration, Distributed constitution, Release by loss of prior governance.
- **Persistence Mode:** Standing, Bearing, Efficacy as modes of achieved availability.
- **54-position state-space:** Cartesian product of three functionally distinct recurring dimensions.
- **Functional independence:** recurrence under substitution inside the grammar, explicitly distinguished from unproven empirical independence.
- **Periodic architecture:** nested 6×3×3 rhythms at the level of generated roles.
- **Coordinate-generated phenomenological syntax:** primary local theoretical object generated before canonical naming.
- **Structural determination:** coordinate constraints define an admissible region.
- **Canonical crystallization:** stable semantically specific realization.
- **Concrete manifestation:** particular instance.
- **Constraint strength / semantic freedom:** mature graded account.
- **Historical formation:** opening, local operations/completions, governing transformation, landing, persistent result; completed form remains distinct from formation history.
- **Sentence history:** privileged observational origin, not ontology.
- **Intelligibility before truth:** exact explanatory priority without replacing truth, correctness, justification, or success.
- **Academic position:** mature functional-phenomenological / cognitive-scientific placement.
- **Internal demonstration:** coherent 6×3×3 architecture, complete 54-position Atlas, one-to-one companion exposition correspondence, mature terminology, repeated internal use.
- **Open hypotheses:** psychological reality, cross-domain recurrence, inter-rater reliability, generalization, exhaustiveness, empirical independence.
- **Not proven:** metaphysical necessity, empirical universality, unique coordinate for every case, causal mechanism, clinical effectiveness, commercial effectiveness.
- **Evidence programme:** blind classification, held-out domains, inter-rater studies, ablation, baseline comparison, human/model disagreement analysis, independent replication.
- **Weakening/falsification:** systematic unrepresentable cases, persistent low reliability, empirical collapse of axes, simpler rival taxonomy, failure to add predictive/discriminative value.

The final essay should make the reader capable of entering the monograph, Atlas, glossary, intellectual history, academic positioning, and Research Status without a conceptual gear change.

**Required final line:**

> **And, of course, we knew all along that you were never five.**

The line reveals the hidden developmental instrument only after the reader has completed the whole ascent.

# VI. Example discipline

Prefer examples already established in the corpus. Reuse is not repetition if the analysis changes.

Core recurring families include:

- red ball / cups;
- key / locked door;
- blocked road / alternate route;
- bridge / support / reach / efficacy;
- classroom becoming quiet;
- toy robot / battery repair;
- bicycle or mechanism repair;
- Lego or bicycle parts becoming a whole;
- decisions and choices settling;
- Atlas examples of keyrings, stars, faces, routes, switches, finalists, channels, finish lines, scopes, fields, gradients, sorting, branches, spans, relations, reciprocity, cycles, and other canonical manifestations;
- sentence-local versus sentence-global completion, reanalysis, conditional organization, attribution, and retrospective stabilization from the monograph/intellectual history.

A strong recurring example should be capable of being described first concretely, then structurally, then compositionally, then formally, and finally epistemically.

# VII. Drafting rule for every edition

Before drafting a yearly essay, prepare a one-page control sheet answering:

1. What can the previous year already understand?
2. What one or two new distinctions are now permitted?
3. How is **every element of the full system** redescribed at this new resolution?
4. Which corpus examples recur from the prior year?
5. Which technical terms remain withheld, which are glossed, and which become fully available?
6. What sentence complexity is permitted by the language calibration?
7. What overclaim must this edition explicitly avoid?
8. What unresolved question should its final movement create for the next year?

No yearly draft is complete if it merely explains its new distinction well. It must also show how that distinction changes the reader’s understanding of the **whole architecture**.
