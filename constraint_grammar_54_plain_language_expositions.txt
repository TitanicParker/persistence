THE CONSTRAINT GRAMMAR OF COMPLETED INTELLIGIBILITY

Plain-Language Expositions of the Fifty-Four Generated Forms



These expositions unfold each form from inside the three constraints that generate it. Each begins with what is unresolved, follows the decisive transformation, shows what makes that transformation sufficient, and then asks what kind of result remains. The three established manifestations are used to reveal the same event-shape across different materials. Only after that structure is clear does each exposition distinguish the coordinate-generated form from its canonical crystallization and return to the companion utterance as the felt compression of the completed situation.



CELL 1

Differentiation / Focal / Standing

Coordinate-generated form: Focal distinguished standing

Canonical crystallization: Focal mark

Companion utterance: "This."

Constraint strength: Near-determinate



Begin before the form has settled. The situation begins with a difference that has not yet been made to count in the required way. The relevant candidates, positions, regions, or degrees may already be present, but the field has not yet articulated the distinction on which completion will depend. Something is still being held together, left unmarked, flattened, or treated as equivalent when the situation requires a difference to become operative.

What changes is that a difference becomes consequential. Differentiation does not merely notice that two things are unlike; it makes a criterion matter to the organization of the field. After the change, the situation can be taken up in terms of a distinction that was not yet governing the opening.

For this change to count as complete, it has to gather around one privileged locus. The relevant locus may be an item, source, target, connection, adjustment point, whole, or endpoint, but the important feature is the same: the field settles by becoming organized around one place of determination. A diffuse or merely distributed version of the change would not yet be this form.

That is the force of Focal completion. One locus becomes the place where the unresolvedness is answered, and the surrounding situation takes its completed sense from that concentration.

What remains afterward is the achieved determination itself. The event can finish and the result is simply there to be taken up: a distinguished item, an available route, an established relation, a corrected arrangement, an organized whole, or a settled result.

This is Standing in its ordinary feel: there it is. The result may later be used, changed, reopened, or embedded in something larger, but what completion leaves most directly is the achieved state or determination itself.

Put together, the three pressures form one event. Differentiation determines the decisive kind of change; Focal determines what makes that change sufficient; and Standing determines what sort of achieved reality remains. The result is not three features laid side by side. It is one completed movement whose most compact domain-free description is already close to this: A difference is concentrated upon one focal candidate until that candidate stands as the distinguished one.

That conjunction matters because changing any one constraint would change the kind of completion involved. If the same transformation instead had to hold across an entire range or system, one privileged locus would no longer be enough; if it completed by release, the decisive fact would be the loss of an earlier governor rather than concentration here. If the result instead survived through an indispensable relation, the bearing would have to remain inside the identity of the completion; if it survived as efficacy, the defining residue would be a capacity or consequence. Here the achieved determination itself is what remains most directly. Changing the Transformation Pattern would be more radical still: it would change what the opening was asking to have altered in the first place. The characteristic feel of this form comes from all three necessities arriving together. In the shortest possible movement, the situation runs from undifferentiated candidate to focal discrimination and lands in distinguished standing.

The first manifestation makes this concrete. Crowded keyring. One key is pointed out. That key stands as the indicated one. The decisive work gathers around one privileged locus rather than needing the whole surrounding field to change in the same way. What is left is the achieved determination itself, available as something that now simply stands.

A second manifestation shifts the material while preserving the same structure. Many stars overhead. An astronomer singles out one star. That star stands as the one meant. The decisive work gathers around one privileged locus rather than needing the whole surrounding field to change in the same way. What is left is the achieved determination itself, available as something that now simply stands.

The third manifestation changes the domain again. Several faces in a photograph. A finger points to one face. That person stands as the identified figure. The decisive work gathers around one privileged locus rather than needing the whole surrounding field to change in the same way. What is left is the achieved determination itself, available as something that now simply stands.

The coordinate-generated form can now be stated without leaning on any one manifestation: A difference is concentrated upon one focal candidate until that candidate stands as the distinguished one.

Only now is it useful to name the canonical crystallization: Focal mark. The name is close to inevitable because it mostly compresses what the generated form, Focal distinguished standing, has already required. This is a Near-determinate form. Once the three constraints are held together, very little structural freedom remains. The canonical crystallization mainly gives a concise local name to an event-shape that the coordinate conjunction has already made highly specific. Concrete materials can vary widely, but the phenomenological syntax has little room to move. What remains free is mainly the domain in which the form appears, the vocabulary used to name it, and minor specification of the local mechanism. Those freedoms do not alter the basic event: the same kind of unresolvedness is answered in the same shape and leaves the same kind of achieved remainder.

What the longer account has unfolded can finally contract back into ordinary speech. The unresolved pressure has changed, the required shape of completion has been achieved, and the right kind of result remains. The whole event can now be felt in the companion utterance:

"This."

The unresolvedness is active: the field still asks for another step before it can be taken up as settled.

What changes is the status of that demand. Settlement does not merely stop activity; it discharges the pressure for further uptake. After the decisive change, the situation no longer owes the same choice, continuation, adjudication, explanation, or completion that organized its opening.

For this change to count as complete, the decisive fact is that something which governed the opening no longer governs it. The prior governor may be conflation, restriction, surface mismatch, faulty organization, imbalance, compulsion, or residual demand. Completion occurs not because the situation merely moves somewhere else, but because the old determination loses its authority.

That is the positive meaning of Release. A new form becomes possible because the earlier way of holding the field is no longer in charge. The completion is therefore an achieved freedom from a specific prior governance, not a vague motion away.

What remains afterward is an accomplished difference in what can happen. The result now works, stops, opens, carries, governs, redirects, functions, or otherwise takes effect. It need not be visibly active at every moment; its defining residue is that a capacity or consequence has been achieved.

This is Efficacy in its ordinary feel: now it works, or now it has taken effect. The completion survives as a changed power of the situation, not merely as something that can be pointed to or described.

Put together, the three pressures form one event. Settlement determines the decisive kind of change; Release determines what makes that change sufficient; and Efficacy determines what sort of achieved reality remains. The result is not three features laid side by side. It is one completed movement whose most compact domain-free description is already close to this: A residual demand for continuation is released completely so that no further action is required and closure becomes an accomplished effect.

That conjunction matters because changing any one constraint would change the kind of completion involved. If the same transformation completed focally, the field would settle around one privileged locus; if it completed distributively, success would have to hold across a constitutive plurality. Here the decisive event is different: the old governor must cease to determine the field. If the result merely stood, completion could be exhausted by the achieved determination; if it survived as bearing, an indispensable relation would be the main residue. Here the decisive remainder is consequential: the field can now do, permit, prevent, carry, govern, or complete something differently. Changing the Transformation Pattern would be more radical still: it would change what the opening was asking to have altered in the first place. The characteristic feel of this form comes from all three necessities arriving together. In the shortest possible movement, the situation runs from residual demand to release and discharge and lands in no-further-action effect.

Only now is it useful to name the canonical crystallization: Continuation discharge. The name is close to inevitable because it mostly compresses what the generated form, Released settlement efficacy, has already required. This is a Near-determinate form. Once the three constraints are held together, very little structural freedom remains. The canonical crystallization mainly gives a concise local name to an event-shape that the coordinate conjunction has already made highly specific. Concrete materials can vary widely, but the phenomenological syntax has little room to move. What remains free is mainly the domain in which the form appears, the vocabulary used to name it, and minor specification of the local mechanism. Those freedoms do not alter the basic event: the same kind of unresolvedness is answered in the same shape and leaves the same kind of achieved remainder.

What the longer account has unfolded can finally contract back into ordinary speech. The unresolved pressure has changed, the required shape of completion has been achieved, and the right kind of result remains. The whole event can now be felt in the companion utterance:

"Nothing further is owed."
