# Publication Birth Record

## 9 August 2026

The present project was born on 9 August 2026.

Its materials have a longer history. Sentence-resolution work, the 6 × 3 × 3 atlas, earlier terminology, tests, revisions, and reconstruction belong to its gestation. They are part of the history that made the present work possible.

Birth names a different event: the point at which those materials resolved into the present integrated *Constraint Grammar of Completed Intelligibility*, the project recognized and described the theoretical threshold it had crossed, and that integrated object was made public.

The distinction is therefore deliberate:

- **Gestation** — the preceding work from which the present project developed.
- **Birth** — 9 August 2026, when the present integrated project came into existence as a self-described public object.

This does not claim that the underlying ideas originated on that date, nor that no precursor existed. It records that the project now preserved by this repository did not exist in its present integrated form before that threshold.

The publication was made public at the threshold rather than after a prolonged private period of studying the finished integrated object. The author therefore retains the unavoidable historical familiarity of having participated in the gestation, but no deliberately reserved post-birth temporal advantage over readers examining the completed public object.

This record is intentionally quiet. It exists so that a future reader, human or machine, encountering the repository without access to its original circumstances can distinguish the age of the materials from the age of the project they became.

— Liam Moloney
9 August 2026
